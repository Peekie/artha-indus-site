There is immense power in singular focus. In this curatorial study, the Warli Tārpā spiral dance, an ancient indigenous celebration of community and cyclical time, commands the entire arrival threshold as an oversized, monochromatic masterwork.

Set against the raw, tactile depth of textured sandstone walls, polished stone flooring, and a minimalist pale oak bench, the high-contrast geometry anchors the space instantly. The centrifugal motion of hand-rendered figures draws the eye inward, engaging the viewer in a moment of calm, rhythmic pause before entering the wider interior.

By pairing master tribal line art with archival conservation framing, our atelier bridges sacred heritage traditions with contemporary architectural scale.

## Architectural & Curatorial Specification Study

- **Specification:** CSI 12 10 00 Art / CSI 12 12 00 Fine Art Framing
- **Medium & Substrate:** High-contrast pigment on 300 GSM acid-free archival cotton rag paper
- **Scale & Proportion:** Custom large-format gallery execution (60″ × 60″ / 1500mm × 1500mm)
- **Framing & Glazing:** Solid matte blackened ash frame with anti-reflective Museum Acrylic (99% UV protection, zero glare)
- **Lighting Integration:** Overhead 3000K narrow-beam track spot (CRI 95+) calibrated for high-contrast legibility without hot spots
- **Sourcing:** Commissioned in direct partnership with Master Warli Artisans with complete ESG provenance and named attribution.
