Where the restorative presence of ancient flora meets modern aquatic architecture. In this hydrotherapy suite study, the vanam composition is embedded into a high-performance architectural resin panel set behind a tranquil vitality pool.

Our vectorization process optimizes linework for high-resolution interlayer printing and laser-etched translucency within cast polymer substrates.

## Architectural & Engineering Specification Study

- **Specification:** CSI 06 61 16 Solid Surfacing / CSI 08 84 00 Plastic Glazing
- **Material & Core:** 0.75″ gauge engineered architectural resin panel with matte frosted surface finish
- **Substrate Compliance:** Class A Fire-Rated, non-porous, mold/mildew resistant, GREENGUARD Gold Certified
- **Fabrication:** High-resolution encapsulation of vectorized artwork within thermoformable polymer resin
- **Lighting Integration:** Submersible and moisture-sealed IP68 backlit LED matrix with adjustable kelvin tuning (2200K–3500K)
- **Sourcing:** Direct artisan partnership with verified fair-wage provenance and named attribution.
