ARTHA | matsya

One motif. Five distinct spatial typologies.

By precision-vectorizing original hand-drawn artwork, Artha Indus Atelier joins ancient craft to modern digital fabrication and commercial specification.

In this spatial study, a single Madhubani Matsya (fish), a traditional symbol of fluidity and abundance, transforms across 5 distinct interior environments:

01 of five

Tactile Residential Relief

Execution: CNC-carved solid American walnut in deep 3D bas-relief.
Impact: Translates fine ink linework into organic wood grain, bringing acoustic warmth and tactile depth to a modern residential salon.

02 of five

Architectural Screening

Execution: Waterjet-cut brushed antique brass screen.
Impact: Functions as a dining room partition, casting dynamic geometric shadows across terrazzo flooring throughout the day.

03 of five

Backlit Grandeur

Execution: Hand-engraved bronze medallion with halo LED backlighting.
Impact: Mounted on dark Venetian plaster to create a striking focal point and an unforgettable sense of arrival in a hotel lobby.

04 of five

Immersive Wallcovering

Execution: High-spec digital acoustic wallcovering.
Impact: Deep indigo, terracotta, and ochre yellow hues transform the linework into a repeating grid that structures room energy.

05 of five

Curated Transitions

Execution: Colorized architectural relief panel on textured plaster.
Impact: Turns a high-traffic elevator landing into a thoughtful, art-led moment of pause.
