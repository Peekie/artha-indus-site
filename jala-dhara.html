We distill centuries of culturally rooted South Asian iconography into disciplined, biophilic visual systems for contemporary architecture.

The Challenge

South Asian artforms carry millennia of sophisticated geometry, organic biophilia, and narrative depth. Yet in their traditional execution, high color saturation and visual density make them difficult to integrate into the restrained palettes of modern architecture.

The Architectural Translation

By isolating the essential stroke, commanding negative space, and working in a calibrated palette, we create art and surfaces that provide grounding, cognitive calm, and timeless narrative resonance across commercial, healthcare, and hospitality environments.

Mithila tradition, Bihar

Kūrma The Cosmic Anchor

Bilateral carapace with concentric radial rings, lotus petal perimeter, and paired focal fish.

Spatial application

Executive boardroom feature lites, private consultation suites, and centered focal door panels.

Glazing translation

Engineered for digital ceramic frit or multi-surface Eco-Etch (Surface 2 or 3). The concentric linework provides 50% to 65% localized visual privacy at seated eye level (42" to 54" AFF) while allowing daylight diffusion across the panel perimeter.

Matsya Aquatic Fluidity

Dynamic undulating silhouette featuring an arched crest of radiating fin-fish and dense, rhythmic concentric scallop scales.

Spatial application

All-over biophilic privacy fields, demountable glass office fronts, and hospitality partition screens.

Glazing translation

The micro-hatched body and scallop scales extract into a continuous, scalable privacy gradient. Calibrated for variable Visual Light Transmittance (VLT from 30% dense coverage to 75% open diffusion), creating optical depth when paired across dual glass surfaces.

Warli indigenous lineage, Maharashtra

Rekhā Terrestrial Rhythm

Minimalist horizontal procession composed of rhythmic triangular human figures, vertical palms, and agrarian transit.

Spatial application

Continuous glass manifestation bands, frameless glass corridors, and sliding entrance doors.

Glazing translation

Modular horizontal repeat engineered for seamless registration across structural glass mullions and butt-glazed silicone joints. Can be executed in crisp matte Eco-Etch or high-opacity white ceramic frit for visibility marking without heavy visual mass.
